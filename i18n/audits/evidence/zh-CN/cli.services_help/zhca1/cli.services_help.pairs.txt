## cli.services_help.reclaim_description  [414 chars, LONG>120]
EN: Ask ONE serve daemon, named by pid, to leave, escalating from SIGTERM to SIGKILL at a bound, after proving the process is this product's serve daemon and that it is not the one serving the address its record names. `lop services status` lists the daemons this is for. It is never automatic: the daemon may be supervising session runtimes, and no reader of a record can prove a successor is ready to take its place.
ZH: 确认进程是本产品的serve守护进程，且不是在其记录地址提供服务的那个进程后，请求由pid指定的一个serve守护进程退出，超时则从SIGTERM升级至SIGKILL。`lop services status`列出适用的守护进程。此操作绝不自动执行：守护进程可能监督会话运行时，仅凭记录无法证明后继进程已准备好接替。
